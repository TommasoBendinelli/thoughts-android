Thoughts for Android — Privacy Policy

App: Thoughts (package org.tbend.thoughts), published by [PUBLISHER]. Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE].

Thoughts is a PDF reader and note-taking app that synchronizes with a Thoughts server that you run or choose yourself. The developer does not operate a service for the app and does not receive your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.

1. Data stored on your phone

Documents you open or import, your comments, highlights, notes and their attachments, review cards and review history, reading positions and app settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Your Thoughts device token and, if you add one, your OpenRouter key are stored encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.

2. Data sent to your own Thoughts server

Only after you enter a server address and device token in Settings → Synchronization, and only when you start a Sync, the app exchanges data with that server over HTTPS. While a server is configured, the app also asks it about every 15 minutes for new "agent notes" (notes you send to the phone from your own computer) and sends your replies. The data exchanged is: documents and source files of your Library, your comments, highlights and notes (with the author name you set, any attachments you add and a place that a comment already has), review cards and review ratings, and changes you make to them. That server is operated by you (or by whoever gave you the device token); the developer has no access to it. To delete this data, delete it in the app and Sync, or delete it on your server.

3. Optional features that contact third parties

  • Read aloud with OpenRouter. If you add your own OpenRouter API key in Settings and use Read aloud, the text being read is sent to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai/privacy), which forwards it to a text-to-speech model, under your own OpenRouter account. Without a key, Read aloud uses the phone's built-in text-to-speech engine and nothing is sent.
  • Importing papers. When you import a paper from a link, the app downloads it and its metadata from the site you named (for example arXiv or OpenReview).
  • Links you open. Translation, web search and other links you tap open in your browser or the corresponding app, which then handle the request under their own policies.

4. Permissions

  • All files access (optional): to list and open the PDFs stored on your phone and save annotations back into them. Files stay on your phone unless you add them to your Library and Sync.
  • Notifications, foreground services, battery-optimization exemption, user-initiated jobs: to show and finish a Sync you started, to keep Read aloud playing, and to serve an offline HTML attachment to your browser.
  • Display over other apps (optional): only for the optional "Study mode", which opens a note sent to you from your own computer.

5. Sharing, selling, children

The developer does not collect, sell or share your data. The app is not directed at children.

6. Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data (except the OpenRouter key and verified source PDFs) in your Google backup, under your Google account's backup settings. The encrypted device token cannot be decrypted on another phone; you enter it again after a restore.
